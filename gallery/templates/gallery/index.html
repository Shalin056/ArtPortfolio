About

I'm a self-taught artist working on paper — no formal training, just a lot of pages. I design costume and armour from the body out: how a plate follows a deltoid, where a seam has to break so the arm can move, what a silhouette reads as at a hundred feet. Blue col-erase to build it, graphite or ink to resolve it, colour last.

Outside of drawing, I work in machine learning and data science. Art is the part of my week that isn't at a keyboard.
